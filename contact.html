Contact The Torque Ledger

By the The Torque Ledger Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every message ourselves, and we are always glad to hear from a fellow enthusiast. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to shop@thetorqueledger.com. Tell us which section you were reading and what is on your mind, and we will do our best to help. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted an error, a wrong figure, or a procedure that needs clarifying? Our editors welcome corrections at corrections@thetorqueledger.com. Accuracy matters in a manual, and we are grateful to be put right.

What we can and cannot do

  • We can explain a general procedure, point you to the right section, or fix a mistake on a page.
  • We cannot diagnose a specific fault from afar, provide the exact figures for your car (your factory manual has those), or recommend particular shops.
  • We offer no paid services, parts, or appraisals, and we will never ask you for payment.

A few common questions

Is The Torque Ledger free?
Entirely. Every section is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Can I rely on your figures for my car?
Treat ours as general guidance only. Always use the correct factory manual for your exact make, model, and year for specifications and torque figures.
A job feels beyond me. What should I do?
Call a qualified mechanic, especially for brakes, fuel systems, and anything safety-critical. Knowing your limits is the mark of a good owner.
Will you add more sections?
Yes, over time, and reader suggestions often guide what we cover next. Tell us what you would find useful.
