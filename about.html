About The Torque Ledger

By the The Torque Ledger Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

The Torque Ledger is an independent manual for the hands-on classic-car owner, written in the clear, methodical spirit of the service manuals that keep these machines alive. It exists for the belief that understanding your car — whether you turn the wrenches yourself or simply want to follow what your mechanic does — is one of the deepest pleasures of the hobby, and that good reference should be free and plainly written.

Our approach

We write methodically and we put safety first, always. Every procedure that carries real risk is clearly flagged, and we send you to the correct factory manual and a qualified mechanic wherever the specifics or the stakes demand it. We explain not just the how but the why, because an owner who understands the reasons works more safely and more confidently. And we keep the register plain and the type large, so the pages serve a reader of any age and any experience.

How we write

Our maintenance notes are general reference, not a replacement for your car's specific manual, and we say so often. Our historical material is checked against reliable references. Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing, and every page carries a "Last reviewed" date so you can judge how current it is.

What we do not do

The Torque Ledger is an independent classic-car maintenance and restoration manual. It is free from top to bottom, sells nothing, and offers no services, parts, or appraisals. When we name a make or part type, it is only to explain. The Torque Ledger is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any manufacturer or brand mentioned. We do not cover emissions-defeat devices or any illegal modification; our interest is in keeping these cars sound, safe, and legal.

Our standing promises

We keep a short list of promises and mean every one. Safety will always come before any repair, and we will always tell you plainly when a job belongs to a professional. Our advice will stay general reference, always deferring to your car's own factory manual for the specifics. The pages will remain free, plainly written, and large-typed. And your visit will be yours alone — we gather nothing about you beyond the text size you choose, which never leaves your own device.

About the photographs

The photographs here are openly licensed images from public collections, downloaded and kept on our own pages rather than borrowed from across the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the photographer beneath the picture, and a full list lives in the site's image credits file.

Say hello

We welcome notes from fellow enthusiasts and mechanics — a tip to share, a question, a topic you would like covered, or a correction we ought to make. The knowledge of our readers is one of the richest resources there is, and we are always glad to learn from it. Write any time to shop@thetorqueledger.com, and see our Contact page for what to expect.
